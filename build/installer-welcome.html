MatiQ Voice

This installer places MatiQ Voice in Applications for all users on this Mac.

Finish recording and choose Quit in MatiQ Voice before continuing. Closing its window keeps it running in the background.

Existing settings, models and recordings are preserved. After installation, open MatiQ Voice from Applications. First-run setup guides model downloads, microphone access and background keyboard permissions.

macOS requires you to approve protected permissions yourself. If replacing a local ad-hoc build, you may need to remove its old permission entry and add the installed app again. The installer does not grant or reset permissions.
